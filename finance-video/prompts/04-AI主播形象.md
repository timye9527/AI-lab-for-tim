# Prompt 04：AI 主播形象与动作

所有提示词基于 [03-AI主播制作.md](../03-AI主播制作.md) 里的人设卡。人物为**虚构**，不要加任何真人名字或"像某某"。

## 定妆照（生图，英文提示词效果通常更稳）

```
Professional portrait photo of a fictional young East Asian woman in her mid-20s,
financial news anchor, oval face, natural makeup, shoulder-length straight dark brown hair,
wearing a navy blue tailored blazer over a white silk blouse, small gold stud earrings,
confident and friendly expression, looking directly at camera,
modern broadcast studio background with soft-focus New York skyline window and stock market screens,
soft key light with subtle rim light, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, photorealistic, 4k
```

变体（每次只改一处，保持其余不变）：
- 机位：`medium close-up, chest up` / `three-quarter view, waist up` / `full body, standing next to a large video wall`
- 服装：`camel turtleneck sweater` / `black pant suit`
- 背景（用于抠像）：`plain dark gray seamless background` —— 生成纯色背景更方便后期合成到统一演播室

负面提示（支持的工具里使用）：
`cleavage, revealing clothing, heavy makeup, extra fingers, deformed hands, text, watermark, logo`

## 角色设定图

```
Character reference sheet of the same woman (use the attached reference image),
consistent face and hairstyle, five views in one image: front, three-quarter left, profile, waist-up, full body,
navy blazer and white blouse, neutral gray background, even studio lighting, photorealistic
```

## 动作（图生视频；输入定妆照）

通用后缀：`static camera, broadcast studio lighting, natural subtle facial expressions, realistic hand anatomy, no camera movement, 5 seconds`

| 编号 | 提示词 |
|---|---|
| M01 | The anchor looks at the camera and talks naturally, slight head nods, blinks naturally, relaxed shoulders |
| M02 | The anchor raises her right hand and points toward the right side of the frame as if presenting a chart, then turns back to camera |
| M03 | The anchor counts on her fingers one, two, three while speaking to the camera |
| M04 | The anchor leans slightly toward the camera with a serious, intriguing expression, as if sharing a surprising fact |
| M05 | The anchor smiles and opens both palms slightly, a gentle "however" gesture |
| M06 | The anchor walks a few steps to the large video wall on her left and turns sideways to present it (camera: slow follow) |
| M07 | The anchor glances down at a tablet in her hands, then looks up at the camera and starts speaking |
| M08 | The anchor frowns slightly and shakes her head, concerned expression |
